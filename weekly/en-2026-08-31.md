## 本周主线 / The Week's Throughline

The week's defining story was a systemic security failure in shared infrastructure. Cosmos Labs urged EVM chains built on its stack to halt operations after a vulnerability drained KiiChain of 148 million tokens, then admitted the bug had been misjudged for four months: reported April 25, deemed low-risk, silently patched May 15, and exploited from August 20 across six networks for nearly $6 million, including MANTRA (~720.9 million tokens moved) and TAC. Roughly 40 networks run the shared Cosmos EVM module, and the response uncovered 11 previously unknown deployments. The episode matters because a single misjudged bug in a shared software layer threatened dozens of chains at once, and the gap between the silent patch and any public advisory left operators unknowingly exposed — silence during patching became part of the failure.

## 持续追踪 / Continuing Threads

- The Cosmos EVM crisis deepened from an urgent halt advisory into a broader disclosure failure. Cosmos Labs contacted 40 networks; 13 potentially exposed chains patched, halted, or applied mitigations, and KiiChain said two of the three underlying defects remain unfixed upstream.
- DeFi losses came from governance and oracle attacks rather than code bugs. Term Finance lost ~$8.5M (2,843 ETH and 1.68M USDC) when an attacker used voting power to pass malicious Meta Vault proposals by zeroing the delay cooldown; the protocol permanently shut Meta Vaults. Moonwell lost ~$8.7M after an attacker spent ~$7M pumping the illiquid MAMO token and borrowed cbBTC, USDC, WETH, and wstETH against it on Base; Moonwell cut borrow and supply caps to 1 wei.
- Coinbase brought equities on-chain at scale. Tokenized US stocks for Nvidia, Apple, Meta, and Alphabet launched on Base with Chainlink price feeds for non-US users; ~$4.5M was minted and ~$3M of DEX liquidity appeared on day one, in a tokenized-stock sector now near $29.5B.
- Solana passed its first binding on-chain governance votes. SGP-0002 doubled the disinflation rate from 15% to 30% with 67.0% support and 60.7% quorum, moving the 1.5% issuance floor from 2032 to 2029; validators also ratified the Solana Constitution (SGP-0001) at 86% and rejected SGP-0003's Resource and Inclusion Fee.

## 值得记住 / Worth Remembering

- Bitcoin's first quantum-resistant mainnet transaction was mined on Aug 26 in block 964,199 using a Winternitz one-time signature via the QSB construction, submitted through MARA's Slipstream with no consensus change. It offers roughly 118-bit resistance against Shor's algorithm, but the ~7 million BTC with already-visible public keys remain exposed, keeping soft-fork migration proposals like OP_CAT relevant.
- The Zilliqa/Ledger incident permanently undermined hardware-wallet trust: a legacy Ledger app discarded eight bytes of entropy per nonce, exposing 6,772 keys and enabling the theft of 683,130,969.66 ZIL in 66 transactions. Since already-published signatures cannot be withdrawn, those keys remain at risk forever; separately, Ledger shipped Ethereum app 1.22.2 to block signing-session replacement attacks.
- Regulatory groundwork accumulated on three fronts: the SEC reopened its Safeguarding Advisory Client Assets rule, which would force investment advisers to hold digital assets with qualified custodians; Treasury's GENIUS Act proposal would require US platforms to audit foreign stablecoin issuers or delist, with comments due Oct 19; and OFAC issued its first sectoral determination covering anyone operating in or servicing Iran's digital-asset sector.
- Deribit moved 90% of client assets to Coinbase Custody and discontinued its daily proof-of-reserves reports — replacing regular public solvency checks with external institutional custody and concentrating a large share of derivatives-exchange assets under a single custodian.
