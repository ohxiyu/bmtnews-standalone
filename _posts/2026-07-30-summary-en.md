## [Two Settings Triple OpenAI's ARC-AGI-3 Benchmark Scores](https://openai.com/index/how-two-settings-tripled-our-arc-agi-3-scores) ⭐️ 9.0/10

OpenAI enabled two API settings—retaining reasoning and enabling compaction—on GPT-5.6, tripling its scores on the ARC-AGI-3 benchmark. This simple configuration change dramatically boosts AI reasoning performance on a key AGI benchmark, suggesting that large efficiency gains are possible without model retraining, which could accelerate progress toward general intelligence. The two settings are 'retain reasoning' \(which preserves chain-of-thought during context compaction\) and 'enable compaction' \(which lossily compresses the conversation history\). rss · OpenAI Blog · Jul 29, 23:00

**Background**: ARC-AGI-3 is an interactive reasoning benchmark that measures an AI agent's ability to explore novel environments, infer goals, and plan actions—skills considered essential for artificial general intelligence. GPT-5.6 is OpenAI's latest large language model, released in July 2026 in three variants \(Luna, Terra, Sol\). 'Compaction' refers to lossy compression of the model's context window, which normally degrades reasoning performance; OpenAI's finding shows that retaining reasoning traces during compaction can actually improve scores.

## [Binance to Remove AEUR Stablecoin, Convert to EUR by 2026](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/40f303353b5f4789aba90dd972990f7e?utm_source=EnglishTelegram&amp;amp;utm_medium=GlobalCommunity&amp;amp;utm_campaign=AnnouncementBot) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Binance announced it will cease support for AEUR tokens on July 31, 2026, and convert all user AEUR holdings to EUR. The issuer, Anchored Coins AG, has stopped issuing new tokens but continues to process redemptions. This move directly affects holders of the AEUR stablecoin, forcing conversion to fiat EUR by mid-2026. It reflects the impact of MiCA regulations on non-EU stablecoin issuers and highlights the ongoing regulatory pressure on euro-pegged tokens. The conversion deadline is July 31, 2026, providing a long transition period. Anchored Coins AG emphasizes that the entire circulating supply of AEUR remains fully backed 1:1 by euro-denominated reserves.

telegram · binance\_announcements · Jul 30, 10:14

**Background**: AEUR is a euro-backed stablecoin issued by Anchored Coins AG, a Swiss company, operating on Ethereum and BNB Chain. Under the EU's Markets in Crypto-Assets \(MiCA\) regulation, stablecoin issuers must be licensed within the EU. As a Swiss entity, Anchored Coins AG cannot comply and has decided to cease issuance and wind down operations, leading to the delisting by Binance.
