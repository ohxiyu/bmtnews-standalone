Strategy \(formerly MicroStrategy\) reported an $8.2 billion loss in the second quarter of 2026, primarily due to impairment charges on its Bitcoin holdings as the cryptocurrency's price declined. This highlights the significant financial risk for companies holding large amounts of Bitcoin under current accounting rules that require impairment charges when prices fall. It could affect market sentiment and the narrative around institutional adoption of Bitcoin. The loss is largely non-cash impairment charges, but it impacts reported earnings. Strategy's substantial Bitcoin holdings make it highly sensitive to price volatility under U.S. GAAP accounting for intangible assets.

rss · CoinDesk · Jul 31, 04:32

**Background**: MicroStrategy, now rebranded as Strategy, has been a major corporate Bitcoin holder since 2020, using its cash reserves to purchase the cryptocurrency. Under U.S. GAAP, Bitcoin is classified as an indefinite-lived intangible asset and must be tested for impairment. When the market price falls below the purchase price, an impairment loss is recognized on the income statement, even if the asset is not sold. This accounting treatment can lead to large reported losses during Bitcoin downturns.
