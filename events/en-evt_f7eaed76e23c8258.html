Current outcome

The European System of Central Banks has recommended scrapping MiCA's requirement that stablecoin issuers hold 30-60% of reserves in commercial bank deposits, instead favoring short-maturity reserve assets, aligning EU rules closer to Britain's systemic sterling stablecoin policy that excludes bank deposits from coin backing.
