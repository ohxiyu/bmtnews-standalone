Current outcome

Coinbase paused deposits and moved the WMTX-USD trading pair to limit-only mode on Coinbase Exchange and Coinbase Advanced following a third-party security incident involving the token; limit orders can be placed and canceled and matches may occur, while market orders cannot be submitted. The asset issuer is actively investigating the issue and updates will be provided as available.
