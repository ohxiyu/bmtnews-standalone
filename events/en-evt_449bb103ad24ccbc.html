    ZetaChain votes to shut down Layer 1 network and move ZETA to Solana

    ZetaChain token holders voted to shut down the project's Layer 1 network and move the ZETA asset to Solana, with the team pivoting to its private AI app Anuma, though the migration requires one more vote.

    Source evidence: The Block

  2. #0209.21
    Confirmation2026-09-21 07:31 · publication time

    ZetaChain holders approve plan to wind down L1, move ZETA to Solana

    Added details: governance proposal 68 passed with 99.4% support and 58% participation, exceeding the 40% quorum; ZETA will become a Solana SPL token via 1:1 conversion with same ticker and total supply; the vote does not immediately trigger shutdown or migration, and core contributors will submit a second proposal detailing the withdrawal window, snapshot block height, shutdown timetable, token claim process, and exchange conversion period.

    State after update: ZetaChain tokenholders passed governance proposal 68 to wind down the Cosmos-based Layer 1 and migrate ZETA to Solana, but the shutdown and migration will not occur immediately; validators continue operating, staking rewards continue, and a second proposal is still needed to detail the migration and shutdown process.

    Source evidence: Cointelegraph
