Current outcome

Thailand's SEC has proposed requiring stablecoin deposits and withdrawals to come from and go to the customer's own verified wallet, with 5 million baht daily inbound/outbound caps per person per operator and a Travel Rule-based waiver for transfers between supervised Thai operators.
