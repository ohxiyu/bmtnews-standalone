Current outcome

The Coldcard Wave 3 exploiter has moved ~45% of stolen Bitcoin via THORChain and CoinJoin; across all waves, 82% of stolen funds remain in original addresses and 18% have been moved for laundering; the operation is ongoing and may have uncovered an additional victim.
