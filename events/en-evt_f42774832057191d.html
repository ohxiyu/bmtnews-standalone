Current outcome

BitMEX has reopened login, KYC and withdrawal access for customers in the US, Canada, Hong Kong, Bermuda, Seychelles and Myanmar — jurisdictions it had previously blocked — so they can recover balances after the platform ended trading operations, while sanctions restrictions and possible account fees for delayed withdrawals still apply.
