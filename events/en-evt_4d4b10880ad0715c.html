Current outcome

Zcash developers unanimously agreed to activate the NU7 mainnet upgrade on Nov. 5, cutting block times from 75 to 25 seconds while keeping daily issuance unchanged via ZIP 218, and integrating the Network Sustainability Mechanism (fee banking starting 2026, supplemental rewards in Feb. 2031), with halvings preserved.

    Zcash targets November for NU7 mainnet upgrade with 25-second blocks

    Zcash developers have reached unanimous agreement to target November 5 for the NU7 mainnet upgrade, which will cut block times to 25 seconds, disable version 4 transactions, and integrate the Network Sustainability Mechanism while preserving the halving schedule.

    Zcash Is Running—Devs Want to Make It Faster
