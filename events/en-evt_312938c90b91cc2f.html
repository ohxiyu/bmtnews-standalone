Current outcome

Celsius's bankruptcy estate sued five BitMEX entities in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York on Sept. 12, seeking about $495 million for 6,360 BTC liquidated during the March 2020 crash; BitMEX is set to stop trading Sept. 23.
