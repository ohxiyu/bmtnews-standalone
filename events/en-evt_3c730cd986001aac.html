Current outcome

Nasdaq invested $100 million in Payward, Kraken's parent company, at a $21 billion valuation, with funds from its venture arm and an extension of a March partnership; Kraken will distribute Nasdaq-listed tokenized stocks carrying full voting rights, and Payward will adopt Nasdaq's surveillance technology across its crypto, equities, tokenized equities, futures, and options venues.
