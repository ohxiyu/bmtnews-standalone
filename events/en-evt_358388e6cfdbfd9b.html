Current outcome

The European Banking Authority recommended that the European Commission extend MiCA to cover crypto borrowing and lending, including crypto firms intermediating access to DeFi lending protocols, and conduct a cost-benefit analysis of adding such services to the regulated list with new compliance and oversight requirements.
