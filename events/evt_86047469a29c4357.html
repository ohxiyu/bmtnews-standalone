    The US Bitcoin reserve faces a crucial bipartisan test Wednesday

    The House Financial Services Committee will mark up the American Reserve Modernization Act (ARMA), which would establish a federal Bitcoin reserve in statute, but the bill has only one Democratic cosponsor who does not sit on the committee.
