Current outcome

Coinbase is opening IPO allocations to eligible U.S. retail customers through its app this week, with smart-ring maker Oura as the first company; Oura plans to list on Nasdaq under ticker OURA, offering 50 million shares at an expected $40 to $44 each. Coinbase said orders do not guarantee an allocation and may be filled in full, partially, or rejected, and selling IPO shares within the first 30 days may bar participation for the following 60 days.
