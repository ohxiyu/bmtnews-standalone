Current outcome

Ethereum and Coinbase-backed Base ended months of negotiations on a common next-generation wallet standard, with Ethereum advancing EIP-8141 for privacy and quantum resistance while Base pursues EIP-8130 for compliance and high-volume flexibility, forcing cross-network wallets to support both.
