    Republicans Post Final CLARITY Act Text With Trump-Backed Ethics Terms a Day Before Cloture Vote

    Republicans released the final CLARITY Act text, revising ethics terms by removing the January 20, 2029 sunset, reversing the July bar on state attorney general enforcement, and raising civil penalties to 20% of the take or $500,000, whichever is greater, ahead of a Senate cloture vote.

  2. #0209.16

    Bitcoin ETFs Had Their Worst Day Since June Following Failed Clarity Act Vote

    The Senate failed to invoke cloture on the CLARITY Act by a 49-50 vote, effectively ending its chances in 2026; the same day U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs saw $450.4 million in net outflows, their worst day since June 24.
