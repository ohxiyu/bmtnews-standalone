Current outcome

The House Financial Services Committee voted 28-21 to advance H.R. 8957, the American Reserve Modernization Act of 2026, which would codify a federal Bitcoin reserve with a 20-year holding period, annual independently audited reporting, and a 180-day study on further purchases.
