    Bitget Hacked as $350 Million Vanishes From Crypto Exchange Wallets

    Bitget confirmed a hack in which roughly $350 million in crypto was drained from its hot wallets, with on-chain data showing about $183 million in ETH, USDT, USDC, AVAX and BNB moved to a single address and a fresh wallet spending $19.67M in USDT0 to buy 7,111 ETH on Arbitrum, while the CEO said cold wallets stayed secure and losses are covered by the exchange's $464M User Protection Fund.

    来源依据：Decrypt · The Block
