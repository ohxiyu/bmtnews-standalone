Current outcome

China's internet regulator is investigating DeepSeek and Moonshot AI over Anthropic's allegations that the two labs secretly routed millions of user exchanges through Claude—over 23 million via 5,380 fraudulent accounts for Moonshot and 12.1 million in a 14-day July window for DeepSeek—to train their own models.
