Current outcome

India's SEBI/RBI "Demat 2.0" pilot is operational, with three issuers raising about $107 million in tokenized corporate bonds on the NSDL/CDSL permissioned ledger, atomic settlement via the wholesale digital rupee, and later phases planned for secondary trading and retail access.

    India Begins Tokenizing Its $620 Billion Corporate Bond Market

    Disclosed the pilot name "Demat 2.0"; three issuers—REC, Larsen & Toubro and IIFL Finance—raised a combined 1,025 crore rupees (~$107 million), with REC first on Sept. 7 raising 500 crore rupees. The system links to the RBI's wholesale digital rupee via a Unified Market Interface for atomic settlement; later phases will add secondary trading and retail access.
