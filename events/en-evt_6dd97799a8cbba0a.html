Current outcome

The US House Ways and Means Committee's Digital Asset Tax Certainty Act (H.R. 10357) has been released and is set for a Sept 16 markup, including a $10 exception for certain fees paid in crypto, along with provisions covering stablecoins, mining, staking, and lending effective in 2028, but omitting a deferral for mining and staking rewards.
