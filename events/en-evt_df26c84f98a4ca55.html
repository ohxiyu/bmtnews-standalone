Current outcome

The ECB is inviting euro-area online and mobile merchants to apply by Oct. 27 for a 12-month digital euro pilot starting in H2 2027, testing beta digital euro payments that will not have legal tender status, with voluntary and unpaid participation.

    European Central Bank calls for merchants to participate in digital euro pilot

    The European Central Bank is inviting euro-zone e-commerce and mobile merchants to join a 12-month digital euro pilot starting in the second half of 2027, testing a beta CBDC ahead of a possible 2029 issuance.
