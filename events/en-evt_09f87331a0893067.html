Current outcome

BitMart's restructuring continues; it missed its Sept. 9 roadmap deadline and has appointed Alvarez & Marsal as financial adviser to evaluate assets and possible paths forward. A user feedback portal is planned within five working days, with further updates expected in the coming weeks.

    Response2026-09-09 13:04 · publication time
