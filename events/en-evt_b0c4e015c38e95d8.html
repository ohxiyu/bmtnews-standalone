Current outcome

Anthropic said it has chosen Accenture as its first embedded evaluator to help slow the pace of AI development as proposed by CEO Dario Amodei on Sept. 12. The partnership is non-exclusive and details of how it will work are still being worked out; Anthropic and Accenture each expect to invest at least $1 billion in the project over the next five years.
