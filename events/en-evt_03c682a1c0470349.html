Current outcome

Nasdaq Ventures has agreed to a proposed $100 million investment in Kraken parent Payward, which plans to deploy Nasdaq surveillance across crypto, equities, tokenized equities, futures and options; the deal is not completed, valuation terms are undisclosed, and NETs remain targeted for Q2 2027.
