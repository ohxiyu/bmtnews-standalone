Current outcome

After the Senate failed to advance the CLARITY Act, the SEC created a five-year conditional exemption for qualifying platforms to trade certain tokenized stocks, the CFTC broadened no-action relief for passive software providers, and the CFTC has now submitted a crypto market rules proposal to OMB for review, with a CFTC vote and public comment still needed before it can take effect.
