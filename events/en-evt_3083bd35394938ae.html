Current outcome

Germany's finance ministry has drafted a bill to impose a flat 25% tax on crypto gains (plus solidarity surcharge), effective January 1, 2027, applying only to assets acquired after that date; assets bought earlier remain under current rules. Automatic withholding by platforms will begin in 2028. The draft is in early coordination within the federal government and could change.
