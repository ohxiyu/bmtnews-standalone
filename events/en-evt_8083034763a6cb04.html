Current outcome

A reported security flaw in Polymarket's identity and authentication flow allowed attackers to gain access to existing user accounts, according to a Gizmodo report. The issue is described as account-takeover-style access rather than an exploit of the platform's smart contracts or market settlement logic. Polymarket is the world's largest prediction market, so account takeover there exposes user balances and open positions directly and undermines confidence in how the platform verifies identity and recovers accounts. It also highlights the security seam that exists on crypto platforms where a KYC/identity layer sits on top of on-chain asset custody. The reported attack path is an identity-layer weakness that lets someone log into an existing account, the classic pattern of an account takeover (ATO) rather than a smart-contract or oracle failure, so the risk concentrates in the account and custody layer instead of the trading engine. The reported flaw concerns access to accounts that already existed, meaning affected users would not necessarily have created anything new or interacted with a malicious contract.
