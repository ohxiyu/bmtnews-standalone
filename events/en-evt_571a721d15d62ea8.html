Current outcome

The Federal Reserve proposed capital requirements, a two-day redemption window, and new reserve disclosures for stablecoin issuers as it implements the GENIUS Act, including a tiered operational-risk capital charge of 2% on the first $20B in outstanding stablecoins, 1.5% on the next $30B, and 1% above $50B.
