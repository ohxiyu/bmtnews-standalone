Current outcome

The U.S. Treasury has sanctioned Iranian crypto exchange BitBank and further designated BitBank-related Pishtaz Simorgh Electronic Trade Company and three Dot One executives under Executive Order 13902 as part of Operation Economic Outcast.

    US sanctions Iranian crypto exchange BitBank over alleged bitcoin transfers to IRGC

    The U.S. Treasury sanctioned Iranian crypto exchange BitBank for allegedly processing bitcoin payments collected by the previously sanctioned Hormuz Safe platform on behalf of the IRGC.
