Current outcome

Chainalysis reports a 420% year-over-year surge in onchain malware, with North Korea- and Iran-linked hackers responsible for roughly two-thirds of new activity; the newly attributed North Korea-linked UNC5342 group uses Tron, Aptos, and BNB Smart Chain as redundant routes, while Iran-linked actors embed command-and-control routing data in Bitcoin transactions, including those sent to a Satoshi Nakamoto-associated address.
