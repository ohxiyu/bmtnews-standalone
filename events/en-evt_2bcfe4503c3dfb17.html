Current outcome

Attackers drained 8.7 million FET, worth about $1.55 million at the time, from SingularityNET’s Ethereum bridge, and 29 minutes later a compromised NuNet minter created 408.5 million NTX sent to the same receiving wallet, according to an on-chain forensic report prepared by Athena. The report said the compromised bridge authorizer and NuNet minter credentials had not yet been rotated during the initial tracking window.
