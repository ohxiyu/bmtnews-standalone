Current outcome

Bitget lost $351.6 million in an overnight hack that CEO Gracy Chen attributed to attackers compromising a wallet backend and spoofing transfer data to trigger the exchange's authorization process — not to stolen private keys — with cold wallets described as secure, withdrawals suspended during a security review, and the $464M+ User Protection Fund set to cover the loss.
