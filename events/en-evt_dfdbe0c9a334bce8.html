Current outcome

Consensys is splitting into two: the existing entity will be renamed MetaMask under Joe Lubin as Chairman and CEO; the new Consensys will focus on Ethereum protocols and institutional infrastructure, led by CEO Mike Kriak, President David Cunningham, and Executive Chairman Joe Lubin. Completion expected by end of 2026.
