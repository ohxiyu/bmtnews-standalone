Current outcome

The attacker behind Bitget's $387.5M breach drained about $83M of stolen XRP from three holding wallets, leaving roughly $75M in accounts that cannot be frozen under the XRP Ledger's rules, while Bitget said its protection fund covers losses and staged withdrawals resume Sept. 28 through Oct. 2.
