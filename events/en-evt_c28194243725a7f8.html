Current outcome

The Federal Reserve opened two public-comment proposals requiring Fed-supervised stablecoin issuers to fully back tokens with safe liquid assets like short-term T-bills and meet standardized capital, risk-management and reserve-safekeeping standards, plus a tailored application process for banks seeking to issue stablecoins.
