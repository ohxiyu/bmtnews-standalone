Rogue iPhone app escapes iOS sandbox to hijack $580,000 in USDT

SlowMist, working with OKX researchers, said FomoPeek versions 1.1 (released Sept.

9) and 1.2 (Sept.

12) distributed through Apple’s App Store contained malware that could escape the iOS sandbox and reach private keys, seed phrases and Keychain data stored by other apps, before the components were removed in version 1.3 on Sept. 17.

The incident has been linked to nearly $580,000 in stolen USDT, with Salus estimating proceeds of about 579,900 USDT and Binance, OKX, Gate, Bitget Wallet and Rabby warning users.

Pragma flags 6 price feeds as critical risk following $3.5M Starknet lending exploit

Pragma classified 6 of 22 mainnet market and rate feeds as critical risk in a Sept.

18 assessment, after a Sept. 17 borrowing exploit at Nostra on Starknet in which a manipulated NSTR oracle price allowed one account to borrow approximately $3.5 million of other assets.

Nostra paused lending, borrowing, withdrawals, and liquidations and said final losses and potential recoveries were still unknown, with the subsequent status of withdrawals and recovery unconfirmed.
