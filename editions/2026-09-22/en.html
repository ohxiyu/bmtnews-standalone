One wallet links $1.55 million FetchAI theft to massive 408.5 million NTX mint

Attackers drained 8.7 million FET, worth about $1.55 million at the time, from SingularityNET’s Ethereum bridge, and 29 minutes later a compromised NuNet minter created 408.5 million NTX sent to the same receiving wallet, according to an on-chain forensic report prepared by Athena. The report said the compromised bridge authorizer and NuNet minter credentials had not yet been rotated during the initial tracking window.

ECB launches ‘digital euro for banks’ and prepares to buy tokenized bonds

The Eurosystem launched Pontes on Monday, giving banks a live route to settle tokenized assets in central-bank money, while the ECB began preparations to invest a small portion of its own funds in tokenized securities settling through Pontes. The ECB’s own-funds holdings were worth €23.1 billion at the end of 2025, with government debt at 73%, but it has not disclosed the size of the tokenized allocation or when purchases will begin.
