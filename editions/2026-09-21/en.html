Why keeping your private keys safe won’t always stop crypto theft

On Sept.

6, Liquid lost almost 4,000 Bitcoin through a withdrawal its software approved even though the private keys used to authorize it had not been stolen; according to TRM Labs' reconstruction, attackers exploited a software flaw to create L-BTC without backing Bitcoin and exchanged those tokens for real coins.

Crypto insurance can cover losses without guaranteeing customers full reimbursement, and even successful repayment can leave customers short if compensation is calculated in dollars instead of Bitcoin.

Why Solana’s new 250ms speed boost could actually trigger network instability

Solana’s 250ms slot time became effective at epoch 1037 on Sept. 18, according to the Solana engineering changelog and a Solana Compass report that placed the transition at about 05:06 UTC; an early Sept.

20 sample covering 60 one-minute windows observed about 266ms per produced slot, and epoch 1037 skipped about 0.05% of scheduled slots.

The source says the more important test is whether leader handoffs, transaction forwarding, repair and multiple validator clients remain reliable as Solana considers a conditional move to 200ms.
