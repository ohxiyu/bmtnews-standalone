## Suggested rubric adjustments

- **Cap scores for community-discussion and routine operational follow-ups at 7.0** unless they surface new verified facts. This covers HN essay retreads and postmortems of already-reported incidents. (Would fix 《复杂系统如何失效》 and GitHub 8月17日宕机事件复盘.)
- **Cap regulatory/executive proposals and aspirational statements at 7.5** until a concrete action occurs (filing, agency response, vote, approval). Keep 8.0+ for rules actually proposed, approvals granted, or incidents with realized impact. (Would fix Robinhood CEO 呼吁代币化股票.)
- **When two stories in the same week describe the same emerging theme from different actors, score the theme, not the individual announcement.** Raise the earliest story to reflect the trend and flag the pair as a thread. (Would fix Binance推出Agent OS and Coinbase推出AI代理交易平台.)
- **Score security incidents by realized impact and threat actor, not by report detail.** Nation-state attacks on critical infrastructure should floor at 8.0, and precursor events (delistings, freezes) that precede a confirmed major event get an upward revision. (Would fix 伊朗关联网络攻击导致英国小型电厂关闭 and retroactively lift 币安下架ICX、SCRT和STORJ.)
