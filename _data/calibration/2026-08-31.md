## Score-rubric adjustments

- **Merge duplicates before scoring, then score the event once.** The same underlying event was scored multiple times at different levels: BitMart restructuring twice at 8.0, Iran sanctions twice at 8.5, Coinbase tokenized stocks five times between 7.5 and 8.0, Solana governance vote three times at 7.5. Use the most detailed version as the base story, and treat confirmations/follow-ups as evidence, not as new 8.0+ stories.
- **Cap single-source, unconfirmed reports at 7.5.** If the story itself says the reporting is secondhand, lacks technical details, or has no official/on-chain confirmation, the default ceiling is 7.5. This would have caught the Bybit 7亿美元 interception (8.0) while preserving the Ledger/Zilliqa story, which was backed by a post-mortem and earned 8.5.
- **Score by structural consequence, not by damage size.** Give ≥8.0 to events that set precedent or change ecosystem structure — first binding network governance votes, court/regulator jurisdictional rulings, custody/transparency regime changes, and multi-chain security exposures — even when the dollar figure is modest (Cosmos at $5.7M scored 9.0; Deribit's transparency change at 7.5 is out of line).
- **Cap routine engineering and product news at 7.5** unless there is confirmed loss of funds, regulatory action, or named-user impact. Cloudflare DNS caching, the seL4 proof, Apple's M6/M5 Ultra launch, and the Qwen/GLM announcements all scored 8.0+ with no follow-up coverage; under this rule they land at 7.5 at most, preserving headroom for events that actually moved the week's news cycle.
