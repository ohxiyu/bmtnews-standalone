    Visa, Credit Coop launch onchain stablecoin credit for card settlement

    Visa announced on Sept. 8 an onchain lending initiative with Credit Coop, an onchain credit protocol that supplies revolving stablecoin-denominated facilities to fund card programs' daily settlement obligations and sweep later cardholder payments toward repayment. Visa says the model has financed more than $2.5 billion in cumulative settlement volume since 2023 with zero defaults. The deal moves a conventional form of receivables finance onto blockchain rails, turning card settlement obligations into collateral and giving Visa's fast-growing stablecoin card business a new short-duration funding channel. It signals institutional adoption of onchain credit infrastructure and expands stablecoin utility from payments into lending and working-capital finance. Smart contracts handle draws, cash-flow control and repayment, with a Credit Coop "Spigot" contract acting as a programmable lockbox that services interest and replenishes the credit line before residual cash reaches the borrower; Visa says Credit Coop has processed more than 3,000 borrow events and 9,000 repayment events. Underwriting still depends on authorized Visa settlement files delivered through a secure pipeline, and the $2.5 billion figure is Credit Coop-supplied with facility-specific loss protections undisclosed.

    Visa Ties VisaNet Data to Onchain Lending for Stablecoin Cards

    Visa announced on Tuesday it is combining VisaNet settlement data with blockchain-based lending infrastructure, allowing lenders to use transaction records to finance stablecoin-linked card programs' working capital needs. Visa cited Credit Coop, which has financed over $2.5 billion in settlement volume since 2023, as an early example. The initiative marks a significant step in institutional adoption by linking onchain credit with mainstream card payment settlement, effectively expanding decentralized lending beyond crypto-native markets. If successful, it could give fintechs and card programs easier access to working capital and deepen stablecoin's role in real-world commerce. Visa reported that stablecoin settlement volume through its network has surpassed a $20 billion annualized run rate, more than 15 times year-ago levels, and that stablecoin-linked payment volume is up nearly 200% year over year across over 160 card programs. The company also noted that onchain lending protocols have processed more than $694 billion in stablecoin loans since 2020, citing Visa's analytics dashboard.
