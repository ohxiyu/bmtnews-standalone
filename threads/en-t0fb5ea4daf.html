    Canopy (CNPY) Lists on Six Major Crypto Exchanges

    Canopy's CNPY token has become available for trading on Binance, OKX, Bitget, Gate, MEXC, and Bitvavo, giving it a simultaneous multi-exchange debut. The listings cover multiple major spot venues and expose the token to a global trading audience. A debut on this many top-tier exchanges, including Binance and OKX, substantially increases CNPY's liquidity, accessibility, and visibility. For Web3 infrastructure projects, such listings are a key milestone for user adoption, although they are also a routine business step for the exchanges themselves. Canopy's website describes the project as an infrastructure layer that lets developers build and deploy on-chain apps with familiar programming languages and tools, while CryptoRank frames it as a framework with restaked security for launching sovereign L1 blockchains. Separate search listings also show another asset named Canopy Finance using the same CNPY symbol, underlining the importance of verifying the official contract address before trading.

    Binance bStocks reach $14.7B trading volume in two months

    Binance reported that its bStocks tokenized stock products have reached $14.7 billion in cumulative trading volume within approximately two months of launch. The milestone highlights rapid early adoption of tokenized securities on the exchange. This milestone signals growing demand for blockchain-based equity products on major crypto exchanges, potentially accelerating the convergence of traditional finance and crypto markets. It also strengthens the case for regulated tokenization as a viable asset class for retail and institutional users. bStocks are tokenized securities backed 1:1 by US shares and were admitted to the FSRA Official List following regulatory approval by the ADGM's Financial Services Authority. This product is distinct from Binance's earlier, discontinued stock tokens launched in 2021.
