    Liquid Network Bitcoin Sidechain Hit by $320M Exploit; Hackers Claim White-Hat Status

    On September 6, 2026, approximately 4,000 BTC worth about $320 million was drained from the federation wallet backing Blockstream's Liquid Network, a Bitcoin sidechain used by exchanges. The network halted all transactions after the exploit, and the attacker or attackers claimed to be white-hat hackers acting as the 'good guys'. This is a major security breach on settlement infrastructure that exchanges rely on, undermining confidence in federated sidechains and custodial models. If funds belonging to exchanges or users were affected, it could disrupt settlement and withdrawal operations for multiple platforms. The exploit targeted the federation wallet that pegs bitcoin to the Liquid Network, moving roughly 4,000 BTC. The attackers described their action as a white-hat operation and reportedly kept about $47 million for themselves, according to one report; the network was halted to prevent further losses.

    Whitehat holding $320M in drained Bitcoin awaits proof of flaw fix

    A bug-created batch of 4,000 L-BTC passed SideSwap's peg-out checks on Sept. 6, prompting the Liquid Federation to release roughly 3,996 Bitcoin (~$320 million) from its reserve. The fund holder later messaged via OP_RETURN that it is a whitehat and will return most funds once Blockstream fixes the underlying Elements bug on every affected node. This appears to be one of the largest security failures on a Bitcoin sidechain, exposing the risk that a federated network can release real BTC when multiple signers approve the same invalid state. Exchanges relying on Liquid for fast settlement paused bridge and L-BTC activity, shaking confidence in sidechain reserve accounting and the broader Bitcoin ecosystem's second-layer security. Liquid said neither SideSwap's Peg-out Authorization Key nor other federation keys were compromised; Blockstream reportedly traced the invalid L-BTC to a flaw in Elements, the software underlying Liquid. At least 11 of Liquid's 15 functionaries signed the transaction, and no independent technical postmortem or patch description was public at the latest check.

    Liquid Network pauses after purported white-hat hackers withdraw $320M bitcoin

    Blockstream has paused the Liquid Network, a Bitcoin sidechain, after purported white-hat hackers withdrew around $320 million in bitcoin. Several exchanges have suspended LBTC deposits and withdrawals, and Blockstream is working to contact those responsible. This is one of the largest security incidents on a Bitcoin sidechain, raising concerns about the security of layer-2 solutions and pegged assets like LBTC. The network pause and exchange suspensions could affect users and liquidity surrounding Liquid Network-based assets, highlighting the risks that remain in trusted sidechain models. The reported withdrawal involved roughly $320 million in bitcoin held on the Liquid sidechain, but the attackers are only "purported" white-hat hackers, meaning their claim of acting ethically to secure funds is not yet verified. The network remains paused while Blockstream contacts the parties involved and investigates the incident.
